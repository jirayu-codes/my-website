Ten classic drinks, one cozy guide. Hover a card to peek at the recipe, and tap the heart to save your favorites.

Hot 🔥

Espresso

A bold, concentrated shot of coffee brewed under high pressure.

Ingredients: Finely ground coffee beans, hot water

Fun fact: A single espresso shot is only about 1 ounce!

Hot 🔥

Latte

Espresso with steamed milk and a light layer of foam.

Ingredients: Espresso, steamed milk, thin foam

Fun fact: The word "latte" means "milk" in Italian.

Hot 🔥

Cappuccino

Equal parts espresso, steamed milk, and thick foam.

Ingredients: Espresso, steamed milk, generous foam

Fun fact: Traditionally enjoyed only in the morning in Italy.

Hot 🔥

Mocha

Espresso blended with chocolate and steamed milk.

Ingredients: Espresso, chocolate syrup, steamed milk, whipped cream

Fun fact: Named after the port city of Mocha in Yemen.

Hot 🔥

Americano

Espresso diluted with hot water for a smooth, bold taste.

Ingredients: Espresso, hot water

Fun fact: Popularized by American soldiers in WWII Italy.

Hot 🔥

Macchiato

Espresso "stained" with a small splash of steamed milk.

Ingredients: Espresso, a dash of steamed milk

Fun fact: "Macchiato" means "stained" or "spotted" in Italian.

Iced 🧊

Cold Brew

Coffee steeped in cold water for 12–24 hours.

Ingredients: Coarse ground coffee, cold water, ice

Fun fact: It's 67% less acidic than hot-brewed coffee.

Hot 🔥

Flat White

Espresso with velvety steamed milk and no foam.

Ingredients: Espresso, microfoam steamed milk

Fun fact: Originated in Australia or New Zealand — the debate continues!

Hot 🔥

Cortado

Espresso "cut" with a small amount of warm milk.

Ingredients: Espresso, equal part warm milk

Fun fact: A staple of Spanish café culture.

Hot 🔥

Irish Coffee

Hot coffee mixed with Irish whiskey and brown sugar.

Ingredients: Hot coffee, Irish whiskey, brown sugar, cream

Fun fact: Created in 1943 to warm up cold airline passengers.
